#Vrinda_Store
## Overview
This Excel project analyzes retail sales data to identify and analyze its 2022 annual sales data to gain customer insights and drive sales growth in 2023.

## Business Questions Answered
1. Compare the sales and orders using single chart
2. Which month got the highest sales and orders?
3. Who purchased more- men or women in 2022?
4. What are different order status in 2022?
5. List top 10 states contributing to the sales?
6. Relation between age and gender based on number of orders
7. Which channel is contributing to maximum sales?
8. Highest selling category?, etc.

#Practice_Sales_Data
## Overview
An end-to-end Excel analysis of a 113,000+ row bike & accessories sales dataset, covering transactions from 2013–2016 across multiple countries. The project demonstrates data analysis, dashboarding, and data-quality auditing skills using native Excel tools
## Datasets
1. Rows: ~113,000 transactions
2. Fields: Date, Customer Demographics (Age, Age Group, Gender), Geography (Country, State), Product Details (Category, Sub
   Category, Product), and Financials (Order Quantity, Unit Cost, Unit Price, Cost, Revenue, Profit)
